How it works

1

Scan the code

Guests scan the QR code at the venue. Nothing to download and no account to make.

2

Search and request

Find any song on Spotify and send it to the queue. Everyone can vote on what plays next.

3

Watch it play

The queue updates live on every phone, the kiosk and the big screen, with a music video for the song.

Everything the venue needs

Music videos

The official video for each song plays on your venue screen, in time with the music.

Kiosk mode

A touch screen where guests search and request, with a QR code when nobody is using it.

Scheduler

Plan what plays and what shows on the screens by day and time.

Family mode

Keep requests, lyrics and videos suitable for every age in the room.

Running an event?

Set up your venue, connect Spotify and share the code. Your guests can be requesting songs in minutes.
